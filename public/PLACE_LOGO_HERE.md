Place your ZKR Company logo in this folder (public/), named exactly:

    zkr.jpg

It's used in the navbar, footer, loading screen, and page metadata
(favicon / Open Graph / Twitter card) automatically via
`components/ui/Logo.tsx` — no code changes needed once it's here.

Recommended: a square image, at least 512×512, ideally with your logo mark
centered (the component crops it to a rounded square).
