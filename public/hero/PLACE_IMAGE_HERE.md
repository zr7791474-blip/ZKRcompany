Place your hero background photo in this folder, named exactly:

    backgroud.jpg

(Yes, that's the spelling used in the brief — the code references this
exact filename, so keep it as-is or update the `src` in
`components/sections/Hero.tsx` if you rename it.)

Recommended: a landscape photo, at least 1920×1080, reasonably compressed
(under ~500KB) so the hero stays fast to load — it's the largest element on
the homepage.
